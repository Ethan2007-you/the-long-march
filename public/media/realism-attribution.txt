chishui-relief.webp
Hillshade and hypsometric shading calculated from Mapzen Terrain Tiles aggregated elevation data.
Public AWS Terrarium data https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Attribution https://www.mapzen.com/rights/
Source-provider attribution https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
USGS SRTM and other applicable aggregated source data. This is a present-day terrain rendering.
Bounds in WGS84 west 105.60, south 27.67, east 106.63, north 28.47. Projection Web Mercator.

River geometry and most place localities © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.

Image replacements — 8 November 2026

Selection: contemporary records first (including eyewitness sketches), later artworks
second, documented present-day photographs third. No AI depiction remains in the
active exhibition. Maps are geographic data renderings, not generated landscapes.
New files below are resized/re-encoded as WebP without retouching or invented detail.
The gallery displays the complete images. The cover and chapter panels crop at display time.
Existing Commons assets retain their original attribution and license records.
